# Privacy Policy for JapSadhana

Last Updated: 2026

JapSadhana ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles user information.

## 1. Information We Collect

JapSadhana does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All app data is stored locally on your device using secure local storage.

This includes:
- Mantra counts
- Daily goals
- Selected deity
- Custom chant text
- Custom deity images
- Language preferences

## 2. Permissions

The app may request access to:

### Photo Library
Used only if you choose to upload a custom deity image.
Images remain stored locally on your device.
We do not upload or share your images.

### Vibration / Haptic Feedback
Used to provide tactile feedback during chanting.

No other sensitive permissions are used.

## 3. Data Sharing

We do not:
- Sell data
- Share data
- Transmit data to servers
- Use analytics
- Use advertising SDKs

## 4. Third-Party Services

JapSadhana is built using Expo and React Native frameworks.
No third-party tracking or advertising services are integrated.

## 5. Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
Since no personal data is collected, the app is safe for general audiences.

## 6. Security

All data remains stored locally on your device.
If you uninstall the app, all stored data is permanently removed.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in future versions.
Any changes will be reflected with an updated date above.
