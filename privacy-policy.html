Privacy Policy

JapSadhana — Your Digital Japa Mala

Last updated: May 2, 2026
JapSadhana is built on trust. Your spiritual practice is deeply personal. This policy explains plainly what data we collect, why, and how it is protected.

The core app — japa counting, mala tracking, history, sankalpa — works completely offline with no account required. Data collection only applies to the optional Community (leaderboard) feature.

📋 Information We Collect

We collect the minimum data necessary to operate the Community feature. Nothing more.

What When Why
Google display name When you sign in to the Community tab Shown next to your rank on the leaderboard
Google email address When you sign in to the Community tab To uniquely identify your account
Google profile picture When you sign in to the Community tab Displayed on your leaderboard entry
Japa chant count While you participate in a challenge To calculate your leaderboard rank. Only tap & auto-japa counts — manual entries are never sent.
Expo push token When you sign in (if notifications are enabled) To send challenge invite notifications
🔒 Your full japa history, deity preferences, sankalpa goals, and app settings never leave your device. They are stored only in your phone's local storage.

🚫 What We Do NOT Collect

No location data No contacts No device identifiers No microphone data No analytics SDKs No advertising IDs No browsing behaviour No in-app behaviour tracking

The microphone permission is used only to record your own mantra for the auto-japa playback feature. Recordings are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

💾 How Your Data Is Stored

On your device: All japa history, settings, and sankalpa data are stored using React Native AsyncStorage — a local, on-device key-value store. This data is never transmitted and is removed if you uninstall the app.

On our servers: If you sign in to the Community feature, your name, email, profile picture, and challenge chant count are stored in a secure MongoDB database hosted on Render (USA). Your JWT authentication token is stored in your device's encrypted secure store (Expo SecureStore).

🤝 How We Use Your Data

  • To display your name and rank on the japa leaderboard
  • To track your participation in naam jap challenges
  • To send push notifications for challenge invites (only if you sign in)
  • To maintain your login session so you don't need to sign in every time

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the app features described above.

🔗 Third-Party Services

JapSadhana uses the following third-party services only when you use the Community feature:

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Expo Push Notifications — for challenge notifications. Governed by Expo's Privacy Policy.
  • Render — cloud hosting for our backend server. Governed by Render's Privacy Policy.

No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are embedded in this app.

👶 Children's Privacy

JapSadhana is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Community sign-in feature requires a Google account, which itself requires users to be at least 13 years old per Google's Terms of Service.

If you are a parent and believe your child has submitted personal information through this app, please contact us at nareshkc03@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

🗑️ Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Delete local data — uninstall the app. All locally stored data is removed.
  • Sign out of Community — tap Sign Out in the Community tab. Your JWT token is deleted from your device immediately.
  • Delete your account — email us at nareshkc03@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Account". We will permanently delete your profile, email address, and all associated challenge scores from our server within 7 days.

🔐 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • All communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS
  • Authentication tokens are stored in your device's encrypted secure store
  • Our backend uses JWT authentication — passwords are never stored
  • Only admin accounts can access user data on the server

📝 Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users through a notice in the app.

Continued use of JapSadhana after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
