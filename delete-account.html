Delete Your JapSadhana Account

Request deletion of your account and personal data

Option 1 — Delete from within the app (recommended)

1
Open JapSadhana on your device.
2
Go to Options (bottom right tab).
3
Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
4
Confirm the deletion. Your account and data are removed within 24 hours.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, or prefer to request deletion directly, send an email to us:

Email nareshkc03@gmail.com

Please include the Google email address you used to sign in so we can identify your account. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, avatar from Google Sign-In)
  • Chant history, streaks, sankalpa, and cloud backup data
  • Community leaderboard entries and challenge progress
  • Notification tokens (push notifications will stop)
  • Any user-uploaded custom deity images stored in your backup

What may be retained (and why)

  • Aggregate, anonymized statistics that no longer identify you (e.g. total community chant counts).
  • Records we are required to keep for legal or fraud-prevention purposes, retained no longer than required.

Timeline

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days of receipt. Data is permanently removed from live systems immediately upon processing; encrypted backups are purged within 30 days as part of routine backup rotation.

Change your mind?

Deletion is permanent. If you'd like to take a break instead, you can simply sign out from within the app (Options → Account → Sign Out). Your data stays safe and you can sign back in anytime to resume your practice.
